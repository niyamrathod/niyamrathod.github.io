Project 06

PL 2026/27
Predictor

A full quantitative football analytics suite built in Python. The core is a Dixon-Coles goals model fitted to four seasons of Premier League results, driving a 10 000-run Monte Carlo season simulation. Five tools sit on top: a Transfer Machine that models the marginal rating impact of any signing; a Smart Signing optimiser that searches the whole player pool for the best affordable signing for a chosen club; a Model vs Market edge board comparing model probabilities to de-vigged bookmaker odds; a Track Record calibration tab with out-of-sample RPS / Brier / log-loss scoring; and live 2026/27 season tracking that will score predictions as results come in from August.

  • Monte Carlo
  • Dixon-Coles
  • Transfer Machine
  • Smart Signing
  • Model vs Market
  • Python
What this taught me about markets

Every tool here maps directly onto S&T skills. The Smart Signing optimiser is a constrained search problem — maximise alpha per unit of budget, the same framing a PM uses for position sizing. The Model vs Market tab is pure relative-value analysis: where does the model disagree with the crowd, and who has been right historically? The Track Record scoreboard applies the same out-of-sample integrity standard that a quant fund would demand — no look-ahead, walk-forward refits only. Building all three showed me that the gap between sport and markets is thinner than most people think.

Best experienced on desktop or laptop.
