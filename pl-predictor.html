Project 06

PL 2026/27
Predictor

Monte Carlo season simulation for the full 2026/27 Premier League — all 380 games, 10 000 runs — built on a Dixon-Coles goals model fitted to three seasons of historical results. The Transfer Machine lets you move any player between clubs and see the formation change, stat impact, and updated win odds for both sides.

  • Monte Carlo
  • Dixon-Coles
  • Transfer Machine
  • Cross-Asset
  • Python
What this taught me about markets

Building the transfer machine showed me how marginal contribution analysis works in practice — the value of moving a player depends not just on their stats but on the positional gap they fill, the cover available to the selling club, and the constraints on the buyer. The same logic underpins how a trading desk prices the marginal risk contribution of adding a position to a portfolio.

Best experienced on desktop or laptop.
