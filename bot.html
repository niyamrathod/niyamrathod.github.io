Project 04

Systematic
Trading Framework

A paper-trading simulation I built to understand how systematic risk management actually works — position sizing, kill-switches, and VaR — rather than just reading about it. It runs on BTC, ETH, BNB, LTC, and XRP using Yahoo Finance data, with no real money involved. The dashboard shows five months of simulated trade history (Jan–Jun 2026) so there's enough data to look at.

  • Python
  • Algorithmic Trading
  • Risk Management
  • Volatility Targeting
  • VaR
  • Strategy Comparison
  • Quantitative Finance
What this taught me about markets

The thing that surprised me most was building five different ways to estimate the same risk number and getting five different answers. I knew the methods existed but I didn't expect the outputs to diverge as much as they did. The strategy comparison also showed results I wasn't expecting: switching approach by market conditions wasn't always the winner month to month, even if it came out ahead over the full period.

Why I built this

I wanted to know what actually happens when you commit to a strategy and run it for months rather than tweaking it in hindsight. The risk controls were the part I was most curious about: whether a kill-switch and position sizing based on recent volatility actually change what the strategy does, or just look good in theory.

What I'd still improve

130 trades over five months is a small sample. Results exclude transaction costs and slippage. The regime-matching outperformance claim holds only in this sample and needs out-of-sample testing before I'd trust it.

Best experienced on desktop or laptop.
