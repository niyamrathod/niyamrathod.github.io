What this taught me about markets

Building the yield curve tab made me realise how much is packed into the shape of the curve. I knew rates mattered but hadn't really thought about what the 2s10s spread is actually telling you about where the market thinks the Fed goes next. The FX carry tab was similar: the concept made sense on paper but seeing the numbers move made it a lot more real.

Why I built this

I wanted somewhere to look at market data each morning without opening five different tabs. The rates and FX tools came from wanting to understand topics I kept reading about but hadn't really seen working in practice: carry trades, yield curve shape, how earnings move stocks.

What I'd still improve

A lot of the outputs are simplified and I'm not fully confident in how to interpret all of them yet. The FX carry numbers don't account for transaction costs or the fact that carry unwinds tend to be fast and correlated, so the backtest is more illustrative than something I'd trade off.

Best experienced on desktop or laptop.
