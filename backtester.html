Project 02

Portfolio
Analysis Suite

Five tools I put together to understand how portfolios actually behave rather than how they look on paper. Stress tests against 10 historical crisis periods, a dual-MA strategy backtester, seasonality analysis, and equity deep dives, all on Yahoo Finance data.

  • Python
  • Gradio
  • yfinance
  • Plotly
  • Portfolio Builder
  • Stress Testing
  • Backtesting
  • Seasonality
  • Quantitative Finance
What this taught me about markets

Stress-testing against historical crises showed that correlations break down exactly when you need diversification most. Assets that hedge each other in calm markets tend to converge in a risk-off sell-off, which was a more uncomfortable result than I expected when I first ran it on a "diversified" portfolio.

Why I built this

I wanted to stress-test the kind of portfolio I'd actually build (a mix of equities, bonds, and gold) and see what happened to it in 2008, 2020, and the 2022 rate shock. The correlation matrix tab was the most useful thing I built: it makes the diversification failure visible rather than just describing it.

What I'd still improve

The backtester uses simple dual-MA rules and doesn't account for transaction costs or slippage, so the results are optimistic. I'm also not confident that 10 historical crisis windows is enough to draw strong conclusions about tail correlation: it's more illustrative than statistically robust.

Best experienced on desktop or laptop.
