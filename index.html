Rates / Fixed Income Open

2s10s DV01-Neutral Steepener

Short-term US interest rates are higher than long-term rates — the opposite of normal. As the Fed stops raising rates, history shows this gap closes fast, with short-term rates falling first. I'm betting on that normalisation.

The steepener is a broadly held macro fund position — crowding is real and the trade has been slow to play out as cut timing stays uncertain. Futures price modest easing but the front end only reprices when cuts actually arrive.

Sovereign Bonds Open

Short OATs / Long Bunds — French Fiscal

France's coalition government has failed to pass a credible budget, and the OAT–Bund spread at 85bps is too tight given the fiscal trajectory. Germany's position is structurally stronger. The driver is French politics specifically — not broad European risk sentiment — which means it behaves differently to other spread trades.

The trade carries some legacy positioning from France's June 2024 snap election — not a virgin idea, and the spread has partially retraced since. Market is pricing moderate French fiscal risk, not a full credibility event.

FX Open

Short USD/JPY — Long JPY

The Bank of Japan is raising interest rates on its own domestic clock — Japanese wage growth and core inflation finally justify normalisation after decades of ultra-loose policy. As the BoJ hikes while the Fed cuts, the rate differential that has kept USD/JPY elevated begins to reverse. The catalyst is BoJ meetings and Japan CPI, not global risk sentiment.

Speculative accounts remain net long USD/JPY despite significant unwind after the August 2024 carry crash — positioning is less extreme but still skewed. Market has priced BoJ normalisation slowly and incrementally, not in a single reprice.

Equities Open

Long XLP / Short XLY — Consumer Stress

US credit card and auto loan delinquencies are running above pre-COVID levels. Consumers are stretching further on less — savings are depleted and real wage growth has stalled. Staples spending is inelastic; discretionary is the first casualty. The driver is household balance sheet deterioration, not broad sector rotation.

Consumer discretionary is consensus overweight in US equity allocations — the soft-landing trade is still broadly held. Staples are under-owned as a defensive, so the long leg of this pair is not crowded.

Digital Assets Watching

Long BTC/USD — Halving Thesis

Bitcoin's April 2024 halving cut new daily supply from 900 to 450 BTC — a programmed supply shock with no equivalent on the demand side. Spot ETF approval added a structural new buyer. The thesis is supply-side, not momentum chasing. BTC's short-term correlation to risk appetite makes entry timing critical — watching for a volatility-driven pullback.

Spot ETF inflows remain net positive but have decelerated from launch-quarter highs — institutional ownership is growing while retail sentiment oscillates. The halving supply thesis is well understood within crypto markets but largely absent from traditional macro allocator frameworks.
