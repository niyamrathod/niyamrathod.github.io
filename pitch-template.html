Field reference

View The thesis. State what is mispriced, the mechanism that closes it, and why now. Make it falsifiable — a specific mechanism, not just a direction.
Instrument Exactly how you express the view. Security, tenor, strike or expiry, and why this structure is better than alternatives for capturing the specific move.
Sizing & Levels Entry, target, and stop in precise terms. A pitch without levels is an opinion. Size should reflect conviction and risk budget.
Risk / Reward Reward divided by risk in the same units. State the breakeven: how far does the market need to move for the trade to break even? Target R/R above 1.5:1 before transaction costs.
Catalyst The specific, time-bound event that moves the market in your direction. Distinguish from rationale (why you're right) — a catalyst tells you when.
What Kills It The bear case. If you can't state it clearly, you don't understand the trade. Knowing what kills it lets you exit quickly when the thesis breaks, not when the pain becomes unbearable.
